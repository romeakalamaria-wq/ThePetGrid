# Sprint 10.4 — Safe Pet Gifts

## Included

- One free gift per member, per pet, every 24 hours.
- Pet owners cannot send gifts to their own pets.
- Memorial pets use their dedicated flower/candle tribute actions.
- Gift creation is validated by a secure Supabase RPC, not only by browser code.
- The pet owner receives a persistent Notification Center alert.
- Gift history shows sender, message, and full date.
- Realtime gift totals remain active.

## Required setup

Run `backend/supabase-pet-gifts.sql` once in Supabase SQL Editor before testing.

# ThePetGrid World Experience — Phase 1: Genesis

## New flagship experience
- Cinematic loading sequence and full-screen space environment.
- Interactive 3D globe powered by Globe.GL/WebGL.
- Night Earth texture, atmosphere, stars and gentle auto-rotation.
- Real Supabase pet coordinates with safe demonstration fallback.
- Animated pet lights, hover labels and smooth camera flights.
- Live world counters for pets, countries and cities.
- World, Pets, Lost and Memorial visual-mode foundations.
- Motion toggle, Lite Mode, responsive mobile layout and WebGL fallback.
- Existing 2D map remains untouched and available as a fallback.
